Why it exists

Every escalation leaks a little.

A payment-failure ticket goes to an outside vendor with a log excerpt attached. Somewhere in it are a card number, a phone, an Aadhaar. Find-and-replace misses one; a blind redactor hides the one thing the vendor needed.

  1. 01

    Manual redaction misses things

    The same address written in two cases, a phone with and without +91 — one slips through.

  2. 02

    Blind tools over-redact

    Everything becomes ****, so the vendor can't tell one customer from another.

  3. 03

    Online scrubbers upload your data

    Pasting a customer's details into a web service to protect them defeats the point.

Features

Precise, transparent, private.

Everything happens on your device, and nothing happens without you seeing it.

Six identifiers, exactly

Email, phone (India-first + international), IPv4/IPv6, PAN, Aadhaar and Luhn-validated card numbers.

Linked review

Select any highlight and its replacement lights up on the other side: “Card 1 of 2 · item 5 of 14”.

Consistent pseudonyms

EMAIL_01 stays the same person through the whole file, even when written differently.

Honest about skips

A card-shaped number that fails Luhn is marked NOT CARD and reported — never silently ignored.

CSV stays intact

Only matched values change. Headers, quotes, CRLF, leading zeros and the BOM pass through untouched.

Nothing leaves the tab

No uploads, no analytics, no cloud AI, and a Content-Security-Policy with connect-src 'none'.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does my text ever leave my computer?

No. Detection and redaction run in the page. The production build’s Content-Security-Policy forbids outbound connections, and the app works offline once loaded.

What exactly does it detect?

Six types: email, phone (Indian mobiles plus + international numbers), IPv4 and IPv6, PAN, Aadhaar and payment cards. It does not detect names or addresses.

Is Aadhaar validated?

It’s a format match (12 digits, first digit 2–9, consistent grouping). The Verhoeff checksum isn’t applied yet, so it errs on the side of redacting more.

Will my CSV still open in Excel?

Yes. Only matched values are replaced; delimiters, quoting, line endings, leading zeros and the UTF-8 BOM are preserved.

Does it use AI?

The redaction uses no AI. In Chrome, an optional button can summarize the already-sanitized result with on-device Gemini Nano — still nothing leaves your machine.
