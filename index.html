    Everything you need to share with a clear mind

    Six identifiers, three ways to replace them, and a review you can trust item by item.

    Six identifiers, nothing vague

    Email, phone (India-first), IPv4 and IPv6, PAN, Aadhaar and Luhn-checked card numbers. Exactly these six — no fuzzy “PII” promises.

    Three gentle modes

    Full mask for [REDACTED], Partial to keep the last 4 digits, Pseudonymize so EMAIL_01 stays the same person all the way through.

    Linked review

    Select any highlight and its replacement lights up on the other side, with a readout like “Card 1 of 2 · item 5 of 14”. Arrow keys work too.

    Honest about what it skips

    A card-shaped number that fails the Luhn check is marked NOT CARD and named in the status line — never silently ignored.

    CSV stays a CSV

    Only matched values change. Headers, quotes, line breaks, leading zeros and the BOM come out exactly as they went in.

    Nothing leaves this tab

    No uploads, no analytics, no cloud AI. A Content-Security-Policy blocks outbound connections, and it keeps working with Wi-Fi off.

    Three calm steps

    Paste, review, share.

    1. 1

      Paste or upload

      Drop in a ticket, a log excerpt or a .txt / .csv up to 1 MB.

    2. 2

      Review every highlight

      Step through each item with Next or ↓ and see exactly what replaces it.

    3. 3

      Download the clean copy

      The file matches the Result pane byte for byte. Send it with confidence.

    Don’t take our word for it — check it

    We’d rather show you evidence than quotes. Each of these you can verify yourself in under a minute.
